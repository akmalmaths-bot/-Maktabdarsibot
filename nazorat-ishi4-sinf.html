1-VARIANT JAVOBLARI VA YECHIMLARI

4-sinf • Matematika nazorat ishi (Tekshirish uchun)

1-VARIANT

1-topshiriq (3 ball)

To‘g‘ri javob

a) 36 405 028

b) 7 308 450 < 7 380 450 512 600 047 > 512 060 047

3-topshiriq. Tenglama (4 ball)

Yechim
48 600 : (x + 15) = 90
x + 15 = 48 600 : 90
x + 15 = 540   →   x = 540 − 15
Javob: x = 525

2-topshiriq. Ifoda (5 ball)

5 bosqich
1) 6 845 − 5 329 = 1 516
2) 1 516 · 25 = 37 900
3) 18 400 − 9 600 = 8 800
4) 8 800 : 4 = 2 200
5) 37 900 − 2 200 = 35 700
Javob: 35 700

4-topshiriq. Masalalar (8 ball)

Harakat
a) Qarshi harakat (uchrashish):
1) Yaqinlashish tezligi: 80 + 100 = 180 km/h
2) Uchrashish vaqti: 900 : 180 = 5 soat
Javob: 5 soatdan keyin
b) Bir yo‘nalishda uzoqlashish:
1) Uzoqlashish tezligi: 65 − 20 = 45 km/h
2) 3 soatdagi masofa: 45 · 3 = 135 km
Javob: 135 km
